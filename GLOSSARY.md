# Asados

A friend group's record of the asados they hold together: who came, what
was cooked and how, and the points each participant earned for it.

## Language

### An asado's status

**Asado Planificado**:
An asado that has been put on the calendar but doesn't count yet: it
earns no points and appears in no standings, totals or charts.
_Avoid_: future asado, draft, pending, borrador

**Por confirmar**:
An Asado Planificado whose date has already arrived but that no member
has confirmed yet. Still earns nothing.
_Avoid_: overdue, vencido, pending

**Asado Confirmado**:
An asado last saved by a member on or after its date, so it really
happened as recorded. Only confirmed asados earn points.
_Avoid_: validated, real, done, realizado

**Confirmar**:
A member saving an asado on or after its date, which declares that it
took place as recorded (after correcting it if plans changed). Saving
one dated today or earlier confirms it at once; saving one dated in the
future makes it planned again. This is the moment its points are
worked out.
_Avoid_: validar, aprobar
